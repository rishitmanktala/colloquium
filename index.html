Diplomacy Begins With Dialogue

Education is no longer confined to the classroom. At Suncity School, Colloquium MUN offers young minds a platform to explore diplomacy, develop empathy, and experience the power of dialogue in addressing complex global issues.

Delegates are invited to think like diplomats, engage like policymakers, and speak like leaders across committee rooms designed for research, strategy, and collaboration.

06

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before the conference.

What is the dress code for the conference?

All delegates are expected to follow Western formal attire. Gentlemen should wear a formal shirt, trousers, tie, and blazer. Ladies should wear formal business attire. National dress is also acceptable.

Is lunch and refreshments included?

Yes, lunch and refreshments are included in the registration fee for both days of the conference.

Do I need prior MUN experience to participate?

Not at all! Colloquium 2.0 is beginner-friendly. We recommend committees like International Press, IMI, or UNFCCC for first-timers. Our executive board members will guide you throughout.

Can I register as an individual delegate?

Yes, both individual delegates and school delegations are welcome. Use the respective registration forms available in the Registration section.

How will country/portfolio allocation work?

Country and portfolio allocations will be shared via email after your registration is confirmed. Preferences submitted during registration will be considered on a first-come, first-served basis.

What awards will be given?

Awards include Best Delegate, High Commendation, Special Mention, and Best Position Paper for each committee. The IPL Auction and International Press will have their own special categories.

Is there a refund policy?

Registration fee is non-refundable once payment is confirmed. However, delegate substitution within the same school delegation is allowed up to one week before the conference.

How do I submit proof of payment?

After making the payment, email a screenshot of the transaction along with your full name, school, and committee preference to colloquium@suncityschool-37d.com.
